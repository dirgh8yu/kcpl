## Direction contract

THESIS: The route is the interface. One crimson line, with the 45-degree cuts of the Gateway K, joins origin to destination through KCPL's staffed places, and the quote form sits on it. Refuses the category default of a stock ship photo, a headline overlay and a row of service cards.

OWN-WORLD: Off-white #F6F6F3 page, ink #101010, crimson #DC143C used as one thick line and whole bands, never as a tint. White station dots with ink rings. Chamfered corners on buttons and plates, cut like the K. Manrope 500 for display, hairlines instead of cards, photographs sparingly.

STORY: The visitor sees their own route drawn, understands one team plans all of it, sees real proof, sees the seven services, then asks for a quote.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Short headline upper left at about 92px, intro upper right. Below, a full-width route diagram with the Origin field at its left end and the Destination field at its right end. Freight mode, a one-line note and the crimson Continue to quote button below it. Phones stack this vertically with the line running between the two fields.

FORM: Transit diagram. Position 1 on the ordered grounded list, chosen by the owner over the assigned position 7 (prequalification dossier). Seed key 843ab2d6. Signature interaction: choosing a freight mode reroutes the whole line. Motion grammar: the line draws once on load, then continues down the page as a spine that fills on scroll, with one station per section.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Raises kept from declined challengers: one control moves the whole line (fold-out sheet); every movement carries its own small route (night bazaar); crimson bands use two inks only (two-ink poster); every control visibly labelled (tape deck); the signature plays once (marbling).

Unresolved: which headline ships. The owner has seen three; B's "One team plans the whole route." is the default until they say otherwise.
